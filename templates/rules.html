OFFICIAL CONTEST CODE

Rules & Regulations

Please carefully review the platform instructions, qualification criteria, timer mechanics, and anti-cheating stipulations prior to beginning the competition.

1. Participant Eligibility & Accounts

  • The competition is strictly open to enrolled undergraduate and graduate students.
  • Each participant must register with their official College Roll Number, academic email, department, and graduation year.
  • Only one active account is permitted per participant. Duplicate registrations will lead to immediate cancellation of all accounts.
  • Sharing login credentials or switching workstations mid-contest without proctor consent is prohibited.

2. Sequential Round Progression System

BUG HUNTERS operates on a strict server-enforced progressive qualification workflow:

Round 1 (Code Quest)
Unlocked by default upon registration. Must score ≥ {{ comp_settings.r1_qualifying_marks }}/15 to qualify for Round 2.
Round 2 (Bug Bounty)
Accessible only after qualifying Round 1. Requires ≥ {{ comp_settings.r2_qualifying_marks }}/25 AND verification by an Administrator.
Round 3 (Logic Launch)
Remains locked until Round 2 is verified. Final results published following verification of Round 3.

Attempting to bypass rounds by manipulating URL parameters will result in an automatic rejection by the server.

3. Timers & Automatic Saving

  • Per-Question Time Limits: Round 1 questions receive 1 minute each; Round 2 problems receive 5 minutes each; Round 3 challenges receive 10 minutes each.
  • Server-Side Authority: The timer begins when the question is loaded and the timestamp is recorded on the server. Refreshing the browser will NOT restore expired time.
  • Automatic Advancement: When the countdown reaches 00:00, the system automatically saves your active response and advances to the next question.
  • No Backtracking: To maintain fairness, once you submit or time out on a question, you cannot return to modify your answer.

4. Anti-Cheating & Tab-Switching Policy

The platform monitors browser visibility changes and focus losses in real time using the HTML5 Page Visibility API and focus/blur triggers.

Warning Levels:
  • 1st Violation: Cautionary warning notification displayed on screen.
  • 2nd Violation: Urgent modal warning indicating severe risk of disqualification.
  • 3rd Violation & Above: The participant account is formally flagged in the proctor audit dashboard for administrative review.

Important Integrity Notice: Browser-based monitoring mechanisms cannot detect external physical devices, dual-monitor displays, or secondary mobile screens. Physical lab proctors will oversee examination venues, and administrators retain full authority to disqualify participants for infractions.

5. Scoring & Tie-Breaking Hierarchy

  • Maximum Total Score: 70 Marks (Round 1: 15 marks, Round 2: 25 marks, Round 3: 30 marks).
  • Tie-Breaking Rule 1: Highest Total Score across all three rounds.
  • Tie-Breaking Rule 2: In the event of a tie, the participant with the higher Round 3 (Logic Launch) score ranks higher.
  • Tie-Breaking Rule 3: If still tied, the participant with the earlier final submission timestamp ranks higher.
  • Finality: All decisions made by the technical evaluation committee and administrators are final and binding.
